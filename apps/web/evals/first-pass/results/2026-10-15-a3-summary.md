## Harness findings

1. **The breaker leaks across wordings.** One basal client serves every
   wording in a run. The 300 ms arm of one wording opens the breaker, and the
   next wording's direct measurement gets no answer at all (`two-way-en`:
   0/255 answered). Each wording here ran in its own process. Fixed in
   #1638: each direct measurement and each arm gets its own client.
2. **The judge's cold start exceeds its 3 s timeout.** The probe call timed
   out on 5 of 12 fresh processes, and 1–4 rows per run hit 3 s on a warm
   process. Each failed probe was retried (at most 3 times, as agreed). The
   production judge has the same 3 s timeout.
3. **Direct basal latency vs the arm on Ollama.** The direct measurement
   reports p50 570 ms, yet the 300 ms arm timed out on only 4 of 255 rows.
   Explained in #1638: Ollama caches prompts, and the arm repeats exactly the
   prompts the direct measurement has just sent (a repeat takes ~45–60 ms, a
   new prompt ~540–730 ms). The arm's Ollama numbers show what a repeated
   message gets; the direct p50 is the one that says whether a new message
   fits in 300 ms.
