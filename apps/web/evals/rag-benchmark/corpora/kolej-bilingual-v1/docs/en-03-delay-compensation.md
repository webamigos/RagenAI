# Delay Compensation Policy — Wolfsbane Interurban Rail

Internal document WIR/POL/2026/03. Effective 1 March 2026.

## 1. Compensation thresholds

Compensation is due to a passenger whose train reached the destination station
late, measured against the timetable in force on the day of travel:

| Delay | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 45 to 104 minutes | **30% of the fare** |
| 105 minutes or more | **65% of the fare** |
| service cancelled | 100% of the fare |

Delays under 45 minutes carry no compensation, whatever the cause.

## 2. Minimum payable amount

Compensation below **EUR 27** is not paid out. A passenger may, however,
combine claims from up to four journeys made within one calendar month and
file a single combined request.

## 3. Exclusions

No compensation is due where the delay resulted from:

- weather conditions declared extreme by the national meteorological service,
- the action of third parties on the track,
- industrial action other than by the operator's own staff.

The burden of establishing an excluding circumstance rests with the operator.

## 4. Processing time

A compensation request is decided within **16 business days**. This is longer
than the deadline for ticket refunds because the actual journey time has to be
confirmed against the traffic management system.

## 5. Form of payment

Compensation is paid by bank transfer or, at the passenger's request, as a
travel voucher worth 20% more than the cash amount. A voucher is valid for
18 months from the date of issue and cannot be exchanged for cash.
