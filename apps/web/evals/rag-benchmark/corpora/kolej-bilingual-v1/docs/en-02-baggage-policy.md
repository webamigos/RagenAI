# Baggage Policy — Wolfsbane Interurban Rail

Internal document WIR/POL/2026/02. Effective 1 March 2026.

## 1. Free allowance

Every passenger holding a valid ticket may carry one item of baggage free of
charge, up to **18 kg** in weight and **147 cm** in total dimensions
(length + width + height).

One piece of hand baggage is carried in addition, free of charge, provided it
fits under the seat or on the rack above the passenger's place and weighs no
more than 6 kg.

## 2. Excess baggage

Each additional item is charged at **EUR 44**. The charge is per item, not per
kilogram, and is not reduced pro rata.

Items over 28 kg are not accepted for carriage under any fare type. The
conductor is required to refuse such an item.

## 3. Items excluded from carriage

Flammable materials, gas cylinders over 4 litres, firearms without a permit,
and sharp-edged objects that are not permanently secured are excluded from
carriage.

## 4. Operator liability

Liability of Wolfsbane Interurban Rail for loss of or damage to baggage is
limited to **EUR 890** per item. The limit does not apply where the loss was
caused by wilful misconduct of an employee of the operator.

A baggage claim must be filed within 10 calendar days of the end of the
journey. Claims filed after that period are not considered.

## 5. Left property

Property left on board is held at the Lost Property Office in Bridgewell for
120 calendar days and is then disposed of under procedure WIR/PROC/2025/08.
