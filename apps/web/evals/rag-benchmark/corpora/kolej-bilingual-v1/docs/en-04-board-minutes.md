# Board Meeting Minutes — Wolfsbane Interurban Rail

Minutes WIR/BOARD/2026/04 of the meeting held on 20 February 2026 at the
company's registered office in Bridgewell.

## Present

Three-member Board. Chaired by the Chief Executive. Minuted by the Board
Secretary. Apologies received from the Technical Director.

## Resolution 1/02/2026 — the "Cockatrice" programme

The Board approved the budget for the rolling-stock modernisation programme
under the working name **"Cockatrice"** at **EUR 2 740 000** for financial
year 2026.

The programme covers replacement of brake assemblies on 9 units of the
class RX-60 fleet and a retrofit of the passenger information system. The
first stage is to complete by **31 October 2026**.

The Director of Fleet Maintenance is accountable for delivery. Progress is
reported to the Board in writing once every three weeks.

## Resolution 2/02/2026 — route B9

The Board resolved to **suspend route B9** (Otterhaven — Thistledown) from
15 June to 15 September 2026 while the Thistledown viaduct is rebuilt.

A replacement bus service will run for the duration of the suspension.
Passengers holding season tickets for route B9 are entitled to a pro-rata
refund, settled under the refund policy but with no handling charge deducted.

## Resolution 3/02/2026 — bicycle tariff

The Board adopted a new bicycle carriage tariff with effect from 1 April 2026.
The single bicycle fare is **EUR 3.40** and the monthly bicycle pass is
**EUR 26**.

The number of bicycle spaces on a class RX-60 unit is unchanged at
**4 spaces** per unit.

## Any other business

The Board noted a 17% increase in refund requests in January 2026 against
January 2025 and asked Passenger Services to produce an analysis of the causes
by 20 March 2026.

The meeting closed and the minutes were signed.
