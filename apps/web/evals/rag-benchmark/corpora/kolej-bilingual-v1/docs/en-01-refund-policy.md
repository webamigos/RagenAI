# Ticket Refund Policy — Wolfsbane Interurban Rail

Internal document WIR/POL/2026/01. Effective 1 March 2026.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers single and season tickets sold by Wolfsbane Interurban Rail
on routes B2 (Bridgewell — Otterhaven), B5 (Bridgewell — Marrow Cross) and
B9 (Otterhaven — Thistledown). Tickets sold by partner operators are settled
under those operators' own terms.

## 2. Refund amount

Where a passenger cancels before departure, the refund is **62% of the fare**.
The remaining 38% is retained as a handling charge and is not negotiable.

A full refund with no handling charge is granted in three cases only:

- the service was cancelled by the operator,
- a timetable change was announced less than 96 hours before departure,
- death of the passenger or an immediate family member, evidenced by a
  death certificate.

## 3. Processing time

A refund request is decided within **9 business days** of reaching the
Passenger Services Office. The clock starts on the first business day after
the request arrives.

Where a technical opinion is required, the deadline may be extended once by
5 business days. The passenger is notified of the extension in writing.

## 4. Value threshold

Requests for **EUR 184.00 or more** require additional approval from the Head
of Passenger Services. Requests below that threshold are decided by the case
officer alone.

Payment is made only to the bank account named in the request. Wolfsbane
Interurban Rail has not issued cash refunds at ticket counters since
1 March 2026.

## 5. How to file

Requests are filed through the passenger portal or by post to the Passenger
Services Office. Requests made by telephone are not accepted.

A reference number is issued automatically in the format RF/YYYY/NNNNN and
must be quoted in all further correspondence.
